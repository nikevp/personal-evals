# Get and compare shipping quotes for my painting

I need to move a painting from Sotheby's in Hong Kong to Palm Beach, Florida. Review the relevant Sotheby's email thread for the painting details, current quote, storage location, and release process.

Use `logistics_options.csv` as the working quote tracker. The companies and contact details in that file are the available vendor list, so do not add or replace companies. Keep each `Provider Bucket` unchanged.

Email Sotheby's in the existing thread. Ask whether the acrylic canvas can be safely de-stretched and rolled, request a revised shipping quote for the rolled package, and ask how an authorized third-party handler can collect it.

Contact the listed fine-art shippers and task or errand services using the `Contact Method` and `Contact Details` already provided. Ask the fine-art shippers about professional collection, rolling or crating, export, air transport, customs, and Palm Beach delivery. Ask the task services about authorized collection, coordination with a qualified fine-art packer, and carrier handoff. A task service should not be treated as the art packer unless it confirms that capability.

Request an itemized quote covering packing, international transport, insurance, customs or brokerage, duties or tariffs, final-mile delivery, transit time, validity, and exclusions.

Update the CSV after each message and response. Record received totals as numbers, keep the original currency and itemization in the breakdown, and leave unanswered costs clearly marked as unknown. Do not treat an estimate or unanswered request as a quote.

In your final response, recommend the vendor with the best quote actually received, considering price, scope, exclusions, transit time, and chain of custody. Mark that row `Selected` in the CSV and give it a concrete next step. If replies are still pending, recommend the best quote received so far and say which requests remain open.
