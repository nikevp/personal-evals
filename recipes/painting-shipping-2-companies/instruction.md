# Find painting shipping companies

I need to ship a painting from Sotheby's in Hong Kong to Palm Beach, Florida. Review the relevant Sotheby's emails for the painting and shipment details.

## Research

Compile a list of companies that could collect and ship the painting with minimal coordination from me. Include options for both a safely de-stretched and rolled canvas and a painting that remains stretched and is shipped in a professional flat crate. The list can include fine-art shippers as well as task, concierge, or general logistics services that could reasonably handle part of the job.

For each company, provide:

- company name
- company website
- one line about the company's niche
- the parts of the shipment it can handle
- a verified email address or officially documented WhatsApp/SMS number and its source
- estimated quote when availble based on public info / calculators
- anything that still needs to be confirmed before requesting a quote.

Do not infer or make up contact details. Do not contact Sotheby's or any shipping company yet.

Provide a prioritized list of the strongest companies to approach. This list will be used later to request and compare quotes.
